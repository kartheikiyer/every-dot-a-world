How a seed becomes a world

Every planet on this site starts as a short string of letters. That seed fixes every random draw the model makes, and everything after the draws follows from published physics, in the order shown in the flowchart.

The chart traces one planet through the model. Each box shows that planet's value at that step; each section below explains what the step does, which branch the planet took, and the relations and papers behind it. Box colours match the tabs on the planet's guide pages.
