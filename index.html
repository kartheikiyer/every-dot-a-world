Every Dot a World

Most of the stars you can see at night have planets. This site invents one at a time, using the same physics astronomers use to make sense of the 6,000-plus real exoplanets, and shows you what it would be like and whether we could ever see it.

  • New planet builds a fresh world. Every world has its own link, so you can share it or come back to it.
  • Drag the planet to look around it, and scroll down for the view from its surface.
  • Why these values? under each section explains the reasoning and the research behind every number.
  • Move the distance slider to see which telescopes, today and in the coming decades, could detect it.
  • Interesting mode favours rarer and more habitable worlds; Realistic shows what a typical planet is like.
